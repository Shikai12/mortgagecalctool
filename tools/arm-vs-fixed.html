About This Tool

Name: ARM vs Fixed Rate Comparison

Scene: Deciding between the stability of a fixed rate and the lower initial payments of an ARM.

Method: Models ARM rate adjustments with caps and compares total costs over your planned hold period.

Interpretation: If you plan to move or refinance before the ARM adjusts significantly, an ARM may save money.

Disclaimer: Future rate adjustments are estimates. Actual market rates will vary.
